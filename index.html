WeirdM

About

WeirdM takes only the first frame of your video and turns it into a WebM whose dimensions change while the original audio keeps playing. Processing happens locally in your browser; your video is not uploaded to a server.

Source code: GitHub.

Diagnostics

When processing fails, copy the complete log and paste it into the bug report/chat. The log stays on this page and is never uploaded automatically.
